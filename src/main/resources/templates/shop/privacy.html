Privacy

MadelMade is a one-person home bakery. This page explains what the site keeps about you and why. Last updated October 2026.

What I collect

  • When you order: your name, email, phone number if you give one, how you'd like to be contacted, your address if you choose delivery or shipping, and the details of your order.
  • If you make an account: your email, and your name if you sign in with Google. Google tells the site only your name and email address, never your password.
  • Anything you send through the feedback button or in messages about an order.

You pay in person or by Venmo or Zelle, so the site never sees card or bank details.

How it's used

Only to bake, confirm and hand off your order, to reply to you, and to keep the records a small business needs (such as sales totals for taxes). I don't sell or share your information, send marketing emails, or use advertising or tracking cookies.

Who else handles it

  • Railway hosts the site and its database.
  • Brevo sends the site's emails, like order updates and sign-in codes.
  • Google handles "Sign in with Google" if you use it.

The site uses one cookie to keep you signed in and remember your cart.

Your choices

Email madelmadewithlove@gmail.com to see, correct or delete what the site holds about you. Order records needed for taxes may be kept for as long as the law requires.
