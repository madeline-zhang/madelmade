How ordering works

1
Build your loaf

Pick a size, flavor and score, and add a birth certificate if you like.

2
Send a request

Choose pickup or delivery and a date. Nothing is charged.

3
Madeline confirms

She approves, or messages you about changes, by your preferred contact.

4
Bake day

You get a note when it's baked, then pick it up or have it delivered.
