About MadelMade

It started with a starter

I'm Madeline, a home baker in Brentwood, Tennessee. Someone gave me a sourdough starter, and I didn't want to kill it, so I kept baking.

One loaf turned into many, and plain loaves turned into designs: leaves, butterflies, names and birthday messages, each scored by hand.

Every loaf is made to order and made to make you smile.

How ordering works

1

Pick a size, flavor and score, and add a birth certificate if you like.

2

3

She approves, or messages you about changes, by your preferred contact.

Frequently asked questions

Anything else? Email madelmadewithlove@gmail.com.

How much notice do you need?

Three days for most loaves.

Where do I pick up?

Pickup is in the Brentwood and Nolensville area.

Do you deliver?

Yes, nearby. Madeline quotes the delivery fee when she approves your order, and it's paid up front as a deposit.

How do I pay?

Nothing is charged online. You pay at pickup or delivery with cash, Venmo or Zelle.

What about allergens?

Every loaf contains wheat. Rosemary garlic asiago contains dairy. Mom's Best is not sourdough. Ask if you have other allergies.

How should I store it?

To enjoy it later, slice it and freeze the slices.

Best way to reheat?

Toast it and butter it up!
